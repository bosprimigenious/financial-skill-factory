Only source-authorized, redacted, versioned materials may be placed in inputs/. Each material needs source_id, owner/publisher, canonical URL or path, capture time UTC, version, SHA-256, byte size, license/redistribution boundary, real/synthetic designation, authorization evidence ID and exact locator.

CSV: UTF-8/UTF-8-SIG, stable headers, ISO-8601 timestamps, explicit timezone, units/currency and null semantics. No nested archives, password-protected files, irrelevant database dumps, duplicates or screenshots in place of structured data.

Private Reference, Ideal Steps, platform Rubric, hidden tests and grader must never be placed in this folder.
